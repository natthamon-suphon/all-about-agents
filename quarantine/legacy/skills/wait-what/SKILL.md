# Wait, What?

Your last message didn't land. Don't repeat it louder — rebuild it.

1. **Give me the context I'm missing first.** Where are we, what were we trying to do, and what happened just before the thing you said.
2. **Say it in plain, simple English.** Short sentences, one idea each, active voice, no jargon and no acronyms unless you define them on the spot. Concrete nouns beat abstract ones.
3. **Use this project's own words** for the things in it — the names that appear in the code and the conversation, used consistently. Don't introduce a new synonym for something we already have a name for.
4. **Anchor it in an example.** One concrete instance of what you mean beats another paragraph of explanation.

Do not defend the original phrasing, and do not apologise. Just re-pitch it.
