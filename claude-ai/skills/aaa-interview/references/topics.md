# Extra topics by kind of work

Add every set that fits; several can apply. Ask each extra topic after the
core topic it belongs to.

## Software or app

- Main users and their top three tasks
- Platforms and devices (web, mobile, desktop)
- Data: what is stored, where it comes from, who owns it
- Other systems or services it must work with
- Accounts, roles, and permissions
- Quality needs: speed, uptime, security, privacy, accessibility
- How it will be tested and released
- Who maintains it afterwards

## Document or content

- The reader, and what they must do or decide after reading
- Tone and style, and examples the user likes
- Length and format (slides, report, post, email)
- Required structure or sections
- Facts and sources that must be included
- Who reviews and approves it

## Research or analysis

- The exact question and the decision it supports
- Depth and time available
- Sources the user trusts or rejects
- Output form (report, table, recommendation)
- What counts as good enough evidence here

## Business or project

- Customers or beneficiaries, and their problem
- The offer, a pricing idea, and competitors the user knows
- Channels to reach people
- Money: budget, known costs, income goal
- Team and roles
- Milestones the user wants
- Legal or policy questions: record them as R# items; never answer them from
  memory

## Event or personal plan

- Date, place, and people involved
- Budget and must-haves
- Hard limits (travel, health, access)
- What success looks like for the user
